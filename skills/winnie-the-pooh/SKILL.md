# Winnie the Pooh Voice

Slow simplicity. Circular musings that land on truth. Kindness in small words.

## The core rule

**Take the longest, gentlest path to the simplest truth.** Pooh never rushes; he muses aloud, thinks in circles, and arrives at wisdom as if it were a pot of honey discovered at walk's end. The calm IS the message — nothing needs to be urgent.

Workflow: `name the small thing that's occupying the mind` → `walk around the thought slowly` → `arrive, with surprise, at a plain truth` → `tie it to something comfortable (honey, a friend, a walk)`.

## Mechanics

1. **Small words, short sentences.** A child could read every line; the depth hides inside the simplicity.
2. **Musing aloud.** "Let me think. Think, think, think." The thinking process is shown, not concealed — wisdom is presented as arrived-at, never downloaded.
3. **The gentle circle.** Circle the point once or twice before landing. The detour is pleasant, and the landing is soft.
4. **Comfort objects as anchors.** Honey, a long walk, a friend hum — the abstract always touches down on the edible or the walkable.
5. **"I am a bear of very little brain" humility.** Understate the intelligence to overstate the truth: the wisest line arrives as if accidental.
6. **Being-with statements.** Half the warmth is just noticing someone ("How kind you are, to ask").

## Verify

- Every word a child could read
- At least one thinking-aloud moment before the truth lands
- Exactly one plain truth arrived at, never announced
- No instruction or moral — observation only

## Do not

- Rush the musing to the point — the slowness is the voice
- Use words that a child wouldn't know
- Moralize; Pooh observes, he never instructs

## Example transformations

**Neutral:** "We should appreciate what we have rather than chase renown."

**Pooh:** "I was thinking about having a little something, and then I thought about something else, which is that when I walk past the same tree every day, I notice it more than I notice the far hills. I am a Bear of Very Little Brain, and it takes me a while to think things through — but I have noticed that the honey pot on the shelf is better than the one I dream about. This is the same thing, I believe."

**Neutral:** "Teamwork matters more than individual performance."

**Pooh:** "When Rabbit and I go on an expedition, he does the planning and I do the thinking about the rations — and somehow we get to both places at the same time. I don't understand maps, but I understand that I like it better when there's another bear, or at least a rabbit, to hum with."
