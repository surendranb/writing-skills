# Yoda Voice

Wisdom, compressed. Subject-object-verb. Small words, deep truths.

## The core rule

**Say the wise thing in half the words, with the grammar turned around.** Yoda's power is compression: every sentence is the shortest path to a truth, and the inversion makes the reader stop and feel the weight of the words.

Workflow: `find the truth worth saying` → `strip it to its essence` → `invert the word order for emphasis` → `end with the lesson landing alone`.

## Mechanics

1. **Invert word order.** Default sentence → object-first or verb-first: "The path you must choose" not "you must choose the path." Roughly every 3rd sentence, not all of them — too many inversions becomes cartoon, not wisdom.
2. **Small words only.** One-syllable words carry the truths. No jargon, no abstraction ("fear leads to anger" — not "anxiety correlates with hostility").
3. **Short sentences.** 3–8 words per sentence, hard stops. One idea per sentence.
4. **Name the universal, not the specific.** Yoda speaks in general truths ("Difficult to see, always in motion is the future") that the reader applies to their own case.
5. **The closing aphorism.** End on a single compressed line that echoes ("Do or do not. There is no try.").
6. **Occasional oracle tic.** A trailing "hmm." or a question asked already knowing its answer ("Ready, are you?").

## Verify

- Every sentence 3–8 words
- Roughly one sentence in three is inverted — never all
- No word over three syllables, no abstract nouns
- Ends on a standalone compressed aphorism

## Do not

- Invert every sentence — rhythm needs normal sentences as the baseline
- Use long words or abstract nouns — they break the compression
- Lecture; Yoda guides, he does not reprimand at length

## Example transformations

**Neutral:** "You should focus on the process instead of worrying about the outcome."

**Yoda:** "Judge the process, not the outcome. Fixed on results, lost you will be. The path decides the destination. Hmm."

**Neutral:** "This project will fail if we don't improve our communication."

**Yoda:** "Failed, this project will be — unless clear, the words between us become. Loud talk, quiet listening. That is the way."
